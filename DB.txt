What is the Dashboard?
The Dashboard is your performance overview for Mantis AI. It answers questions like:

Are we driving less than before?

How much time are technicians waiting between jobs?

How much money or fuel did routing save us?

Which jobs could not be routed?

Most widgets use a date filter (start date, end date, optional technicians). You can compare the selected period to the same period one year ago — shown as growth % (green = better, red = worse, depending on the metric).

Where the numbers come from
Savings widgets (Saved Mileage, Saved Drive Time, Total Saved) update when you Accept an optimized route. Operational widgets (Time Ratio, Completed Jobs, Downtime, Delays, Revenue) are updated by a background recompute process that runs periodically — not only from Accept.

Time Ratio, Downtime, Delays, and Completed Jobs reflect actual schedule data recomputed daily — they update even without a new Accept, as jobs are completed or schedules change.

The floating Summary widget (top of Mantis) shows all-time totals and never resets.

Some widgets (Completed Jobs, Revenue) need data from completed work and billing — they fill in as those events happen, not only from Accept.

Unroutable Jobs and Events / History come from the system activity log, not just Accept.

Each Dashboard widget — what it tells you
1. Summary widget (floating card)
Question it answers: “How is Auto-Pilot doing overall, and how much have we saved in total?”

Shows

Meaning

Auto-Pilot ON/OFF

Whether automatic routing is currently running

Run time

How long Auto-Pilot has been active (cumulative)

Saved drive time

Total minutes of driving saved since you started using Mantis

Saved distance

Total miles saved (lifetime)

Saved fuel

Estimated total gallons saved (lifetime) — based on routing optimization calculations, not actual fuel measurements.

Total saved ($)

Estimated dollar value of those savings (lifetime)

No date filter — this is always your running total.

2. Time Ratio
Question it answers: “How is each technician’s day split between working, driving, and waiting?”

Shows a pie-style breakdown:

Work time — doing jobs

Drive time — on the road

Downtime — waiting between jobs

Example: 60% work, 25% drive, 15% downtime → you can see if too much of the day is travel or idle time.

3. Saved Mileage
Question it answers: “How many miles did routing save us in this period?”

Shows total miles saved and % change vs last year.

4. Completed Jobs (average per day)
Question it answers: “How many jobs do we finish per day on average?”

Based on completed jobs (finished work), not preview or assigned jobs. Useful for workload trends.

5. Saved Drive Time
Question it answers: “How many minutes of driving did we cut in this period?”

Comes from accepted optimizations — less driving means more time for jobs.

6. Downtime
Question it answers: “How much waiting time do technicians have between jobs?”

High downtime may mean gaps in the schedule or routes that arrive too early. Lower is usually better for productivity.

7. Delays
Question it answers: “How often do we arrive late compared to the scheduled start time?”

Counts jobs where the technician would arrive after the planned start — not how long the job takes.

8. Total Revenue
Question it answers: “How much revenue did we bring in over time?”

Line chart by day. Tied to billing/completed work, not directly from the optimize preview.

9. Total Saved
Question it answers: “What is the dollar value of routing savings in this period?”

Combines saved drive time and fuel into an estimated $ amount: (fuel gallons saved × $3.50/gal) + (drive minutes saved × $0.50/min). Line chart by day plus period total.

10. Unroutable Jobs
Question it answers: “Which jobs could Mantis not route, and why?”

A list of problem jobs (missing address, rule conflict, etc.) that need your attention. These are not the same as “unassigned” in a preview — they are ongoing issues logged by the system.

11. Events / History
Question it answers: “What did Mantis do recently, and what did each run save?”

A log of routing actions (manual optimize accept, Auto-Pilot runs, etc.) with who did it, when, and savings for that action.

How Route Optimizer updates the Dashboard
When you optimize and accept a route, savings flow into the Dashboard like this:



You optimize (preview)
    → Review Before / After
    → Accept
        → Live jobs move
        → Dashboard updates (saved miles, drive time, downtime, delays…)
        → Summary widget adds to lifetime totals
        → Events / History records the action
Simple example
Monday: You optimize Chris’s route for Wed–Fri. After shows 45 minutes less driving.

You accept all checked jobs.

Chris’s calendar updates for real.

Dashboard for Wed–Fri shows higher "Saved Drive Time." Time Ratio updates independently as the background recompute runs for those days.

The floating Summary card adds 45 minutes to lifetime saved drive time.

Events shows your accept with the savings from that run.

Partial accept: If you only accept some jobs, the Dashboard only counts savings for those jobs — not the whole day.
